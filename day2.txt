Dataset Creation & Evaluation Plan
1. Dataset Categories
We plan to build a diverse dataset covering three key sources:
Body-Worn Camera (BWC) recordings
Podcasts
News channel debates
2. Key Observations & Attributes to Capture
A. Audio Quality & Environment
Background noise types (traffic, crowd, indoor, etc.)
Noise levels and pitch variations 
Accent variations across speakers
B. Speech & Transcription Metrics
Word Error Rate (WER)
Transcription Error Rate
Speaker Diarization Error Rate
C. Conversation-Level Attributes
Speaker proximity:
People speaking near vs far
Overlapping speech:
Frequency
Duration
Conversation challenges:
Interruptions
Crosstalk
Clarity issues
D. Acoustic & Interaction Features
Voice pitch variations
Signal quality / distortion levels
Temporal characteristics of speech segments
E. Emotion & Behavioral Signals
Emotion detection categories:
Crying
Shouting
Screaming
Neutral / calm
Additional behavioral cues (tone, intensity, stress)
3. Next Steps – Solution Evaluation
Once dataset and observations are established:
Evaluate solutions for:
Speech-to-Text (Transcription)
Speaker Diarization
Emotion Detection
Benchmark performance across all categories (BWC, podcasts, debates)
