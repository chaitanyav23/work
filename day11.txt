  Key Observations:

   1. The "Deletions" Trap in 10B: 
       * In Phase 10B (Denoised), the Deletions (91) are more than double those of Phase 10C (44). This confirms that DeepFilterNet is
         "eating" words, which directly causes the WER to spike from 30% to 37.6%.

   2. The "Confusion" Fix in 10C:
       * Phase 10C (using NeMo MSDD) reduced Speaker Confusion from 23.6% (10A) down to 10.4%. This is the primary reason the cpWER
         improved so drastically.

   3. The 10B Paradox:
       * Interestingly, Phase 10B has the lowest WDER (10.1%). This means that when it does manage to recognize a word, it assigns it to
         the correct speaker very accurately. However, because it misses so many words entirely (high deletions), its overall cpWER
         (53.0%) is still worse than Phase 10C (50.3%).

   4. SA-WER is the Winner:
       * Phase 10C achieves the best SA-WER (43.8%), proving that the combination of Normalized Audio + MSDD Diarization + Midpoint
         Fusion is the most reliable architecture for BWC forensics.
