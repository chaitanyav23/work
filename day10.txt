 > but why does it also help in removing all the unnecessary noise of wind and other artificats and unnecessary things from the audio   
                                                                                                                                        
✦ Actually, loudness normalization does NOT remove noise on its own. In fact, if you just normalize a noisy audio file, you often make
  the background noise (wind, sirens, static) even louder and more noticeable.

  The reason the Phase 5 results were so clean is because of how normalization interacts with the neural models that follow it in the
  pipeline. It helps "remove" noise through three indirect mechanisms:

  1. Lifting Speech Above the "Model Floor"
  Neural networks like Whisper and Pyannote have a mathematical "floor"—if a signal is too quiet (e.g., -40dB), the model assumes it is
  just electronic noise or silence and ignores it.
   * The Problem: In raw BWC audio, the Subject is often near that floor. 
   * The Fix: By normalizing to -20dB, we lift the Subject's speech into the "Active Zone" of the neural network. This allows the model
     to clearly distinguish the spectral patterns of speech from the random patterns of wind noise.

  2. Standardizing the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)
  Neural models are trained on datasets where speech is at a consistent level. If your audio is too quiet, the "ratio" of speech to the
  model's internal processing noise is poor. Normalization optimizes this ratio, allowing the model's internal filters to work as
  designed. It’s like looking at a photo in a dark room vs. turning on a light; the "noise" (shadows) is still there, but your eyes can
  now see the "signal" (the object) clearly.

  3. Improving the "VAD Cleaning" Logic
  When the audio is normalized, the Voice Activity Detection (VAD) becomes much more reliable:
   * High Sensitivity: Because the audio is boosted, the VAD can see the "edges" of words more clearly.
   * The "Muting" Trick (The actual noise removal): Once the VAD identifies the speech, we mute (set to zero) everything else. 
   * Result: This is where the noise removal actually happens. Normalization allows the VAD to be accurate enough that we can safely
     delete the wind and sirens in the gaps between words without accidentally deleting the speech itself.

  Summary: The "Cleaning" Partnership
   * Normalization: Turns up the lights so we can see what is speech and what is noise.
   * VAD: Marks the "trash" (the noise) for removal.
   * Muting Logic: Actually throws the trash away.

  In Phase 5, because we used human-vetted segments (Oracle VAD), the "cleaning" was perfect. In Phase 7, we use normalization to ensure
  our 0.05s Soft VAD is powerful enough to do that cleaning automatically without missing the quiet words.
